Questions, answered.

Where do the trailers come from?
Trailers play via YouTube's official embedded player inside Apple's secure in-app browser. We don't host or modify trailers — we curate the feed and learn what you love.
Is it really free?
Yes. v1 is free with no ads. We may offer optional Pro features later (custom playlists, themes), but the core loop will always be free.
Why is auto-advance timer-based instead of "when the trailer ends"?
YouTube's player runs inside Apple's in-app browser, which doesn't expose a "trailer ended" callback for compliance reasons. The 90-second cycle is intentional — most trailers run 60–90s anyway, and you can always swipe to advance manually.
Why don't you track what I watch?
Because we don't need to. The taste profile that powers smart shuffle lives on your device. There's no backend that holds your data, no advertiser asking for it.
