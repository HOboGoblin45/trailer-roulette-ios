Trailer Roulette

Privacy Policy

Effective date: TBD · Last updated: 2026-04-25 · Contact: crescicharles@gmail.com

Plain English: We don't collect anything. Everything stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Trailer Roulette stores all preferences (watchlist, taste profile, filter settings, app state) locally on your device using Apple's standard Preferences storage.

We do not run analytics on you, do not have user accounts, do not require login, and have no backend that holds your data.

Third-party services

ServiceWhat's sentWhy
TMDB API key (the app's), search/filter parameters Movie metadata (titles, posters, descriptions)
YouTube (via SFSafariViewController) The trailer URL the user tapped YouTube serves the player; YouTube's privacy practices apply during playback
Apple App Store / TestFlight Crash reports and aggregate usage stats (Apple-mediated) Per Apple's own privacy policy

None of these services receive personally identifiable information from us. We never identify you to TMDB or to any third party.

What we don't do

  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No advertising IDs (no IDFA usage)
  • No selling, sharing, or transmitting any personal data
  • No third-party SDKs that fingerprint or profile users
  • No push notifications (no notification token to collect)
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access

App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

Data Not Collected. All data types under all categories.

Children

Trailer Roulette is not directed at children under 13. Movie and TV trailers may include content (violence, language, mature themes) per the source studios' classifications. We rely on the App Store age rating; parents should use Screen Time / Family Sharing for additional controls.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. There is no server-side data to request deletion of.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last Updated" date and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be called out in the app's "What's New" notes at next release.
