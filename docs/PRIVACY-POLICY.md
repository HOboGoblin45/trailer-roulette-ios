# Privacy Policy — Trailer Roulette

**Effective Date**: TBD (set at App Store submission)
**Last Updated**: 2026-04-25
**Contact**: crescicharles@gmail.com

## Plain-English summary
We don't collect anything. Everything stays on your device.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Trailer Roulette stores all preferences (watchlist, taste profile, filter settings, app state) locally on your device using Apple's standard Preferences storage.

We do not run analytics on you, do not have user accounts, do not require login, and have no backend that holds your data.

## Third-party services
- **TMDB (The Movie Database)** — we query their public API for movie metadata (titles, posters, descriptions). The API key is stored in the app; no personally identifiable data is sent to TMDB. *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.*
- **YouTube** — when you tap play, the trailer opens in an in-app web view (Apple's `SFSafariViewController`). YouTube's own privacy practices apply during playback. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- **Apple App Store / TestFlight** — Apple may collect crash reports and aggregate usage stats per their own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## What we don't do
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No advertising IDs (no IDFA usage)
- No selling, sharing, or transmitting any personal data
- No third-party SDKs that fingerprint or profile users
- No push notifications (no notification token to collect)
- No location access
- No camera, microphone, or photo library access

## App Store privacy nutrition label declaration
**Data Not Collected.** All data types under all categories.

## Children
Trailer Roulette is not directed at children under 13. Movie and TV trailers may include content (violence, language, mature themes) per the source studios' classifications. We rely on the App Store age rating; parents should use Screen Time / Family Sharing for additional controls.

## Data deletion
Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. There is no server-side data to request deletion of.

## Changes to this policy
We'll update the "Last Updated" date and post the new version at the same hosted URL. Material changes will be called out in the app's "What's New" notes.
