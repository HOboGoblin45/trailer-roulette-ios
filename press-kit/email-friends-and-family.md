# Email — friends & family soft launch

Send this 24h after the App Store goes live in your soft-launch country (Canada or Australia per `docs/SUBMISSION-CHECKLIST.md`). Personalize the opener for each recipient.

## Subject lines (pick by audience)
- (movie people) "I made a thing — would you try it?"
- (developer friends) "Six weeks of weekends → my first iOS app"
- (broad list) "Trailer Roulette is live. 60 seconds, then ignore me."

## Body

```
Hey [name],

After about six weeks of solo nights and weekends, my movie-discovery app Trailer Roulette is live in the App Store.

It's a slot machine for trailers. Spin through curated trailers, save what you love, swipe past what you don't. The more you swipe, the smarter the next spin gets — and everything stays on your phone (no accounts, no tracking).

Two favors, if you're up for it:

1. Try it: https://apps.apple.com/app/trailer-roulette
   (Free, no ads, no signup. ~60 seconds to know if you'd use it.)

2. Tell me what's missing. I'm planning a v1.1 Couple's Mode (two people swipe, app shows what you both loved). Anything else you'd want?

Either way — thanks for being on the receiving end of this email. I needed a low-stakes thing to ship and this turned into something I'm actually proud of.

— Charlie
```

## Send mechanics
- BCC, not CC, when sending to a group
- Send in waves of ~20 — single-shot 100-recipient blasts often hit spam filters
- Track in your task manager which lists got the email and when

## Replies — what to do
- "I'd use it but it's not in my country yet" → great signal. Note for the global rollout. Personal reply.
- "Found a bug" → file in `docs/bugs.md` immediately, reply with "filed, fixing in next build"
- "I love it" → ask if they'd post a Reddit comment / App Store review (gently!)
- "Why didn't you build [X]?" → ask follow-up questions; this is your real user research
