# Product Hunt — launch assets

## Launch day plan

1. Schedule for **00:01 PT on a Tuesday or Wednesday** (best traffic, away from launch-day floods on Mon/Thu/Fri).
2. Have at least 3 friends ready to upvote in the first hour.
3. Be in the comments all day — Product Hunt rewards founder responsiveness.

## Tagline (60 char limit)
```
Shuffle through movie trailers like channels. Save what you love.
```

## Description (260 char limit)
```
A slot machine for movie trailers. Spin through hand-picked trailers, save the ones you love, and let the app build your taste profile on-device — every swipe sharpens what comes next. No accounts, no tracking.
```

## First comment (the founder's intro — paste 30s after launch)

```
Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Charlie. I built Trailer Roulette because choosing a movie has gotten harder, not easier. Streaming services know what's *new*, but they don't know what's *for me*.

So I made a slot machine for trailers. Spin → swipe right on what you loved → swipe left on what you didn't → the app builds a taste profile on your phone (nothing leaves the device) → future shuffles bias toward what you'll likely love.

Three things I'd love feedback on:
1. Does the 90-second cycle feel right, or too short/long?
2. Is "Couple's Mode" (two people both swiping; intersection of likes) something you'd use?
3. What's missing from v1 that would make you reach for this over the YouTube app?

Tech: Capacitor + React, trailers play via YouTube's official embedded player. No backend, no analytics, no ads. v1 is free.

Happy to answer anything!
```
