# What's New — v2.9.0

For the App Store Connect "What's New" field on the v2.9.0 update submission.

```
Trailer Roulette is now beautifully simple. Two buttons: Play and AirPlay.

• Press Play. A random trailer comes up — any genre, any decade. Press again for a fresh surprise.
```

## Why this messaging

Leads with the single biggest user-visible change (the immersive, lean-back
"channel" experience), then the new swipe interaction, the all-eras hook, and
AirPlay. Nothing about the engineering (the redesign, the removed filter/search
UI, the playback architecture) — App Store readers care about what's different
on screen, not the internals.

> **Accuracy guard:** do NOT reintroduce copy about Search, genre/decade
> filters, a Classic/Modern toggle, or a "learns what you love" taste profile.
> Those were removed in the v2.5 redesign and are not in this build. See
> `research/why-this-app-is-original.md` for the current, accurate feature list.
