# What's New in v3.2.0

**Theater Mode.** Tune the roulette to a real independent theater. Tap the new Theaters button, pick a theater near you, and the roulette spins only what's actually showing there this month — new releases, restored classics, and the weird midnight stuff. Tap Everything to go back to the full random channel spanning all of cinema.

**Playback fix.** Trailers no longer get cut off after a few seconds when YouTube runs a long pre-roll ad. Every trailer now plays to its real end.

As always: two buttons, no accounts, no algorithm.
